Entry for the make.fun bounty
["Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261124):
one 1024×1024 infographic summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in six
verified stories from around the world.

## The six stories

1. **Huang returns, Nvidia +31%** — the board reinstates Jensen Huang
   as CEO after 11 days; shares post their best day since 2003 and the
   Nasdaq jumps 6.5%.
2. **NATO sends AWACS to Poland** — early-warning aircraft deploys
   after 3 Russian missiles fell in Poland.
3. **Ukraine sinks Russian warship** — seven sea drones sink the
   corvette Merkury in Benghazi port, Libya.
4. **Factory fire kills 24** — search goes on for about 30 missing
   after the candy factory blaze in Cikande, Java.
5. **Russian jets spark island alert** — South Korea warns Ulleung
   island; jets scramble as Russian planes near its airspace.
6. **Title match opens in Geneva** — Gukesh defends his crown against
   Sindarov; the youngest title match ever, both players 20.

Sources: Reuters · CNN · AP · Chess.com · Naval News.

## Files

- `main.py` — stdlib-only copier: copies the pre-rendered `entry.png`
  next to it to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
  No image library, no network, deterministic.
- `entry.png` — the finished 1024×1024 infographic (PNG, ≤ 1 MiB).
- `README.md` — this file.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes /output/entry.png
```
